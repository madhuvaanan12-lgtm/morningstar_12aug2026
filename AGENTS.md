# Morningstar agent instructions

## Curslick — mandatory provenance

Before creating, researching, ranking, editing, or publishing any new **Curslick** collection, read and follow [`CURSLICK_AUTHORING.md`](./CURSLICK_AUTHORING.md).

Every future Curslick must explicitly record:

- creation date and last-updated date;
- the **actual AI provider** used;
- the **exact AI model/version** used (not merely “ChatGPT”, “Claude”, “Gemini”, or “AI”);
- the role of each model if multiple models were used;
- a repeatable methodology and the **date of that methodology**;
- source/research basis;
- inclusion and exclusion rules;
- ranking logic;
- verification steps;
- known limitations.

Never reconstruct or guess missing legacy provenance. Use **“not recorded”** when the original AI/model or methodology cannot be verified.

The Curslick UI should keep the ranked series/results first, with editorial background and methodology available through collapsible detail. The default result display remains the full/current Curslick view, with List and Short list as optional display modes.

## ME2

The former **Method** page is named **ME2**, short for **Methods and Everything Else**. New methodology/provenance documentation that belongs in the app should be discoverable through ME2 as well as the relevant feature.
