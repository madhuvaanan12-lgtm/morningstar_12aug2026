Trope study · global television

Hired to protect them,
kept for good

One premise, told everywhere: a dangerous, competent man attaches himself to a woman's household as bodyguard, minder or nanny. Her children want him gone. Something tries to hurt them, he stands in the way, and by the end the kids call him theirs and she does too. Nineteen shows below, sorted by how completely each one actually delivers the beat you're asking for — the near-perfect fits first, the famous near-misses last, with the traps flagged.

03 · PROOFThe threat arrives. He puts his body between it and them.
04 · TURNThe children adopt him first. The mother is always last to admit it.
05 · FAMILYMarriage, or a shot of the four of them leaving together.
